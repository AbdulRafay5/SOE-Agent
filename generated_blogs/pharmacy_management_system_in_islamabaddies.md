## Challenges Businesses Face Without This  

### 1. Inefficient Inventory Management  

#### Stockouts and Over‑stocking  

Manual stock logs often result in inaccurate counts, leading to frequent stockouts of high‑demand medicines and costly over‑stocking of low‑turn items. This not only erodes profit margins but also damages patient trust when essential drugs are unavailable.  

#### Expiry Management  

Without automated alerts, expired drugs can sit on shelves unnoticed, resulting in legal penalties and potential health hazards.  

### 2. Regulatory Non‑Compliance  

#### Record‑Keeping Obligations  

Pakistani law requires pharmacies to maintain detailed records of every dispensed prescription, especially for Schedule III–V drugs. Failure to do so can attract fines up to PKR 500,000.  

#### Reporting to DRAP  

Monthly reports on controlled substances must be submitted electronically. Manual compilation is time‑consuming and prone to errors, increasing the risk of non‑compliance.  

### 3. Poor Customer Experience  

#### Long Waiting Times  

Cash‑based, paper‑centric workflows force pharmacists to spend excessive time on billing and inventory checks, extending customer wait times.  

#### Lack of Personalized Service  

Without a digital patient profile, pharmacists cannot easily track medication histories, allergies, or refill reminders, limiting the ability to provide personalized counseling.
