## Real‑Time Inventory Synchronisation  

The heart of automation lies in instant stock updates. When a sale occurs—online or offline—the following sequence executes automatically:  

1. **POS registers transaction** – The barcode scanner records the SKU and quantity sold.  
2. **Inventory engine deducts stock** – SadaHisab decrements the on‑hand quantity in its central database.  
3. **API push to Shopify & WooCommerce** – The updated stock level is sent via REST API to each platform.  
4. **Online storefront reflects new quantity** – Customers see the correct available stock, preventing overselling.  

If an online order is placed, the process runs in reverse: the order is pulled into the POS queue, stock is reserved, and the physical store can fulfil the order directly from its inventory.  

Low‑stock alerts are configurable; when a product falls below a defined threshold, SadaHisab sends an email notification and displays an on‑screen warning, enabling proactive re‑ordering.
