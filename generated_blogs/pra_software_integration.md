## What is the Pakistan Revenue Authority (PRA)?  

The Pakistan Revenue Authority is the federal body responsible for tax collection, customs, and enforcement of fiscal policies across the country. In recent years, PRA has rolled out an e‑invoicing framework that obliges registered businesses to generate digitally signed invoices and submit transaction data through an online portal. Many business owners specifically search for a reliable PRA Software integration to solve these exact challenges.

The authority’s official services include:  

* **e‑Invoicing gateway** – Receives XML‑based invoices from registered vendors.  
* **Taxpayer Self‑Service portal** – Allows businesses to view submitted data, check compliance status, and request refunds.  
* **API documentation** – Provides developers with endpoints for creating, updating, and querying invoice records.  

Understanding PRA’s requirements is the first step toward seamless integration, because the software must translate local sales terminology into the standardized fields the authority expects (e.g., GST rate, taxable amount, buyer’s CNIC).

## What is Digital Invoicing in Pakistan?  

Digital invoicing, also known as electronic invoicing (e‑invoicing), replaces paper receipts with machine‑readable documents that are automatically stored and transmitted to tax authorities. In Pakistan, the system mandates:  

* A unique invoice number generated by the seller’s software.  
* Inclusion of the buyer’s Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) when available.  
* Digital signature or QR code that verifies authenticity.  

When a POS system like **SadaHisab** generates a digital invoice, it simultaneously creates the XML payload required by PRA’s API. This dual output satisfies both the customer’s need for a receipt and the regulator’s demand for a compliant electronic record.

## How It Works or How to Get Started  

Integrating PRA with **SadaHisab** follows a straightforward sequence. Below is a step‑by‑step guide that any SME owner—or their IT consultant—can follow.  

### Step 1: Register for PRA e‑invoicing  

* Log in to the PRA self‑service portal.  
* Apply for an e‑invoicing license by submitting your business registration documents and Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN).  
* Once approved, you will receive API credentials (client ID and secret).  

### Step 2: Enable the Tax Module in SadaHisab  

* Navigate to **Settings → Integrations → Tax Authorities** inside the SadaHisab dashboard.  
* Select “Pakistan Revenue Authority” and enter the API credentials obtained in Step 1.  
* Choose the tax regime (e.g., GST, Sales Tax) that applies to your industry.  

### Step 3: Map Your POS Fields to PRA Schema  

* Use the built‑in field mapper to align your product codes, unit prices, and discount structures with the XML elements required by PRA (e.g., `<ItemCode>`, `<TaxableValue>`).  
* Test the mapping with a few sample invoices to ensure correct data formatting.  

### Step 4: Perform a Sandbox Sync  

* PRA provides a sandbox environment for trial runs.  
* Click “Sync Test Data” in SadaHisab; the system will send a batch of dummy invoices to the sandbox endpoint.  
* Review the response log for any validation errors and adjust the mapping as needed.  

### Step 5: Go Live  

* Once the sandbox test passes, switch the integration mode to “Production.”  
* All future sales will be transmitted automatically to PRA’s live API.  
* Set up email or SMS alerts within SadaHisab to notify you of successful submissions or rejections.
