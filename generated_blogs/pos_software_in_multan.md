## What is Digital Invoicing?  

Digital invoicing is the electronic generation and storage of sales invoices, as opposed to hand‑written or printed paper copies. In Pakistan, the FBR encourages businesses to issue digital invoices to simplify tax filing and reduce paperwork. A digital invoice contains mandatory fields such as the seller’s NTN, buyer’s details, tax amount, and a unique serial number that can be verified online.  

When **POS Software in Multan a digital invoice, it automatically assigns the correct tax rate (e.g., 17 % GST), records the invoice in the system, and stores it in the cloud for easy retrieval. ## What is Inventory Management in a POS?  

Inventory management within a POS tracks the quantity, location, and movement of every item you sell. In a Multan grocery store, it records when a bag of rice is sold, when a fresh batch of mangoes arrives, and when items near their expiry date need to be discounted. The system can generate low‑stock alerts, batch/expiry tracking, and automatic re‑order suggestions based on historical sales patterns.  

Because the inventory data lives in the same cloud database as sales and purchases, you never have to reconcile two separate spreadsheets. When a sale is recorded, the stock count drops instantly; when a purchase order is received, the stock count rises. This real‑time visibility prevents over‑stocking, reduces waste, and ensures you always have the right products on the shelf for your Multan customers.
