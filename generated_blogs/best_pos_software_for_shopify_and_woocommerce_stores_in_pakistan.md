## 1. Why Integration Matters for Dual‑Channel Sellers

When a product sells on Shopify, the same SKU may also be purchased on a WooCommerce store or in a brick‑and‑mortar outlet. Without a unified POS, each channel maintains its own stock ledger, leading to:

* **Overselling** – customers encounter “out of stock” messages after payment.
* **Pricing drift** – promotional discounts applied on one channel do not reflect on the other.
* **Complex reconciliation** – manual spreadsheets are required to align daily sales reports, increasing the risk of accounting Best POS Software for Shopify and WooCommerce Stores in Pakistan in Pakistan** must therefore act as a single source of truth for product master data, handle Pakistani sales tax (GST and provincial rates), and generate compliant digital invoices that can be stored for the statutory 5‑year period required by the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR).

## 2. Core Integration Capabilities to Verify

### Real‑Time Stock Synchronization
A POS that pushes inventory changes instantly to both Shopify and WooCommerce prevents double‑booking. Look for APIs that support webhook notifications for stock‑level changes, as well as batch sync options for high‑volume catalog updates.

### SKU Mapping and Variant Handling
E‑commerce platforms use different conventions for product variants (size, color). The POS must allow custom field mapping so that a Shopify variant ID aligns with a WooCommerce attribute without manual intervention.

### Order Lifecycle Management
From order creation to fulfillment and returns, the POS should capture every stage and push status updates back to the e‑commerce stores. This includes:

* Auto‑generation of fulfillment IDs.
* Return authorizations that adjust stock automatically.
* Refund processing that mirrors the original payment method.

### Tax Engine Adapted to Pakistan’s tax structure includes a federal General Sales Tax (GST) of 17 % and province‑specific rates for services. The POS must calculate tax per line item, apply correct GST codes, and embed tax breakdowns in digital invoices that meet FBR standards.

### Multi‑Branch and Multi‑User Support
If the business operates several physical locations, the POS must consolidate sales across branches while preserving individual cash registers for accountability. Role‑based access control ensures that staff can only view or edit permitted data.

### Reporting Aligned with Local Accounting Practices
Financial statements, profit‑and‑loss reports, and cash‑flow summaries should be exportable in formats compatible with popular Pakistani accounting software (e.g., QuickBooks Pakistan, Xero) and support Urdu language if required.

### Phase 1 – Requirements Gathering
* List every sales channel (Shopify, WooCommerce, physical stores).
* Document product attributes, tax categories, and discount rules.
* Identify required user roles (cashiers, managers, accountants).

### Phase 2 – POS Configuration
* Set up company profile with PKR as base currency.
* Input GST 17 % and any provincial tax rates; enable digital invoice template that includes FBR‑required fields (TIN, invoice number, tax breakdown).
* Create barcode schema for fast scanning in-store.

### Phase 3 – SKU Mapping
* Export product catalogs from Shopify and WooCommerce as CSV.
* Align SKU codes; resolve variant mismatches by creating a mapping table (e.g., “SH‑TS‑001‑RED” ↔ “WC‑TS‑001‑R”).
* Import the unified list into the POS; verify that each variant appears correctly on both e‑commerce dashboards.

### Phase 4 – Sync Activation
* Enable webhooks on Shopify and WooCommerce that trigger stock updates on the POS.
* Set up POS‑to‑shop sync so that every sale reduces inventory across all channels instantly.
* Test return flows: process a return in the POS, confirm that the stock level increments in both Shopify and WooCommerce.

### Phase 5 – Pilot and Validation
* Run a 7‑day pilot in a single store. Monitor:
  * Stock discrepancy reports.
  * Tax calculation accuracy on digital invoices.
  * Latency of order status updates.
* Collect feedback from cashiers and adjust user permissions if needed.

### Phase 6 – Full Deployment
* Replicate the configuration to all remaining branches.
* Schedule a brief training session for staff; provide SOP documentation covering daily reconciliation and troubleshooting.

| **Insufficient staff training** | Transaction errors, lost sales | Conduct role‑based training; maintain a quick‑reference guide near each register. |
