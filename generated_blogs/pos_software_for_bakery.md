## Misaligning the POS with Bakery‑Specific Inventory Needs

A bakery’s inventory is fundamentally different from that of a grocery store or a clothing outlet. Flour, sugar, and butter are measured in kilograms, while finished products are counted by piece. Many bakers install a generic POS and immediately discover that stock levels do not reflect the real situation, leading to over‑purchasing, waste, or missed sales. Choosing the right POS Software for Bakery can directly impact daily efficiency and long-term growth.

**How the mistake occurs**

- Selecting a system that only tracks items by unit count, ignoring weight‑based ingredients.
- Ignoring batch and expiry tracking, which is critical for food safety compliance.
- Failing to configure low‑stock alerts for raw materials that have short shelf lives.

**Prevention strategy**

1. **Map every ingredient and finished product** in a spreadsheet, noting the measurement unit (kg, litre, piece) and typical usage per batch.  
2. **Configure SadaHisab’s inventory module** to handle both weight‑based and count‑based items. The platform allows you to set up separate stock‑keeping units (SKUs) for raw materials and for each bakery item, linking them through a recipe or “bill of materials” feature.  
3. **Enable batch/expiry tracking** for perishable inputs. The system will automatically flag ingredients approaching their use‑by date, prompting you to adjust production schedules.  
4. **Set low‑stock thresholds** for each raw material. SadaHisab sends real‑time alerts to mobile devices, enabling timely re‑orders without manual checks. Many business owners specifically search for a reliable POS Software for Bakery to solve these exact challenges.

## Ignoring Mobile and Offline Capabilities

Bakery stalls at fairs or pop‑up locations often lack stable internet. A POS that requires constant connectivity can halt sales entirely in such scenarios.

**Pitfall**

- Deploying a cloud‑only solution without offline caching.

**Mitigation**

- Choose a POS that **stores transactions locally** when the network is down and synchronizes automatically once connectivity returns. SadaHisab’s mobile app supports offline mode, ensuring uninterrupted service at temporary venues.  
- Test the offline switch‑over before the event to confirm that receipt printing and payment processing (via card reader) continue to work.
